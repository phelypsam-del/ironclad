Ironclad Duel

A pixel-art knight fighting game. Swing your sword with the mouse, break your enemy's armour piece by piece, and fight your way across medieval Europe.

⚔ Play now ⬇ Download

Steel, mud and blood

Your mouse is the sword

The cursor is your sword point. Circle, cut, thrust, wind up for an overhead or pull back for a lunging stab. Faster swings hit harder.

Real armour

Plate dents, cracks and shatters. Mail tears. Find the gaps, break an arm and watch your foe fight on one-handed.

Blades that clash

Swords bind, scrape and beat each other aside. A held point keeps a man at bay until he knocks it out of line.

Shields

Bucklers, round shields, heaters and tower shields. Hold right click and steer your shield to catch blows, or shove with it.

Stamina

Every swing costs breath. Spend it all and you drop to your knees, open to anything.

Online duels

Make a room, send the four-letter code to a friend and fight them live, right in the browser.

Fight across Europe

Start as a peasant with a rusty sword and a homespun tunic. Win gold, buy better steel in the armoury, and fight through fifteen towns in five realms of the year 1350.

  • Kingdom of Englandpeasants and outlaws
  • Kingdom of Francetown militia in mail
  • Holy Roman Empiremercenaries in part plate
  • Teutonic Ordercrusading knights
  • Byzantine Empirethe Varangian Guard

How to fight

Mouse
Move the cursor: your sword point follows it. Swing through your opponent fast to hit.
Circle behind your head
Overhead cut.
Pull back, then push
Lunging thrust.
Right click
With a shield: move your shield. Without one: reverse grip, using the crossguard like a hammer against plate.
Double click
Kick your opponent back.
A / D
Step back and forward.
W / S
Raise or lower your shield.
Space
Dodge.
