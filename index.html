How to fight

Mouse
The cursor is where your sword point goes. Move it anywhere: circle it to turn the blade, sweep it through him to cut. Faster is harder.
Overhead
Cursor up and behind your helm, then over and down onto him.
Stab
Bring the cursor in close to pull your hand back with the point on him, then drive it out at him. Tap left to lunge.
Right button
Reverse grip: the crossguard becomes a hammer.
Move
A / D walk. Space or the wheel steps back.
Shield
Hold W to raise it, S to lower it. It stays where you leave it.
Campaign
Free five towns on the road to the capital, fifteen fights each. Win gold and spend it in the armoury.
Power
Only speed hurts. A slow touch does nothing; a fast, full swing bites deep and dents plate.
Kick
Double-click left to boot him backwards. Little damage, but it knocks the wind out of him.
Stamina
Every swing, stab and block costs breath. Empty the bar and you drop to your knees, helpless, until it fills again.
Armour
The figure by each name turns from green to red as plate and mail are destroyed. Bare cloth bleeds.
